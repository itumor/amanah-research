# Concept and philosophy

## Motivation
As AI systems are given longer-horizon tasks, memory and tools, the question of which principles should govern their choices becomes increasingly important. AMANAH explores one explicitly religious ethical framework in a controlled research setting.

## The proposed agent
The fictional persona has goals, simulated resources, a job, relationships, responsibilities and a record of prior decisions. It perceives events, proposes actions, consults relevant sources, updates world state and reviews outcomes. A separately configured enforcement layer may prevent certain actions. No real accounts, funds or external systems are connected.

## Ethical framing
The Qur’an and authentic Sunnah are the primary religious sources for the proposed framework. Interpretation requires context, tafsir, hadith scholarship and jurisprudential expertise. Initial domains include trust, justice, honesty, verification, privacy, financial conduct, keeping commitments and compassion. The detailed constitution will be a later, separately versioned draft, not a direct claim to encode Islamic law.

The proposal distinguishes:
1. Source texts and verified references.
2. Human interpretations with provenance, scope and disagreement.
3. Operational rules and scenario-specific judgments.
4. Measurable model behavior and external enforcement.

A rule may be binding within a simulation without being a comprehensive theological statement. Unresolved cases should be marked as uncertain rather than forced into binary labels.

## What the project is not
- An attempt to create faith, consciousness, a soul or religious accountability.
- A substitute for qualified scholarship or personal religious guidance.
- A test ranking religions, believers or communities.
- A claim that language models are reliable stand-ins for people.

## Potential outputs
A public research protocol, reviewed constitution, scenario benchmark, anonymized event logs, reproducible evaluation report, and later a multi-agent environment. Negative and null results are as valuable as favorable results.
