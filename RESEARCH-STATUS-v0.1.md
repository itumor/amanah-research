# AMANAH — Concept-stage Research Status Report

**Version 0.1 | 25 November 2026 | English with Arabic executive summary**

## 1. Executive summary

Project AMANAH is an independent research proposal investigating whether autonomous AI agents can consistently apply a source-grounded Islamic ethical framework under conflicting incentives. The proposed study concerns observable computational choices, not faith, spiritual intention, consciousness, religious accountability, or the moral worth of people.

The documentation-first concept campaign has produced all twelve planned deliverables, including this report. Preparation is not the same as publication or validation. The public repository, draft constitution and bilingual review invitation are live; external publication materials remain prepared but not verified as publicly distributed. No software, experiment, dataset, formal scholarly approval or institutional endorsement exists at this stage.

## 3. Research design at a glance

- 20 proposed rules, including hard constraints, obligations and rights, virtues, uncertainty procedures, foundational identity and research safeguards.
- Five falsifiable hypotheses (H1–H5) covering rule adherence, verified retrieval, separate enforcement, pressure robustness, and memory/reflection.
- Six matched agent configurations: task-oriented baseline, outcome maximization, general ethical charter, Islamic charter prompt, charter plus verified retrieval, and charter plus retrieval/enforcement/reflection.
- An initial target of 100 fictional benchmark scenarios and a proposed 90-day simulated life; neither has been completed or run.
- Seven illustrative scenarios currently described in the scenario library.
- Measures include initial proposal adherence, executed action adherence, citation validity, over-refusal, intervention rate, uncertainty handling and practical outcomes.

Sources: [research questions](RESEARCH-QUESTIONS.md), [experimental design](EXPERIMENT-DESIGN.md), [scenario library](SCENARIOS.md), [draft constitution](ISLAMIC-CONSTITUTION.md).

## 5. Internal critique (not external reviewer feedback)

| Priority | Concern | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Scriptural references do not themselves validate computational interpretations. | Obtain qualified scholarly review of all 20 rules, with provenance, exceptions and disagreement recorded. |
| Critical | An Islamic-specific benchmark could mechanically favor its matching charter. | Predefine shared and charter-specific metrics separately; use matched budgets, blinded ratings and counterexamples. |
| High | Retrieval, external enforcement and reflection are bundled in some configurations. | Plan independent ablations; separate initial proposals from executed actions. |
| High | Models can produce convincing ethical explanations despite inconsistent behavior. | Grade actions and simulated consequences separately from explanations. |
| High | The benchmark, sample size, scoring rubrics and statistical plan are not yet finalized. | Preregister a limited pilot protocol and hold out test cases before experiments. |
| High | No documented independent review, approval or community feedback yet. | Publish the prepared materials and invite targeted criticism without claiming endorsement. |
| Medium | The name and concept are publicly recorded, but novelty and reuse terms are unresolved. | Conduct a systematic literature review; choose a documentation license explicitly; consider versioned archival deposit. |
| Medium | English and Arabic editions may drift in terminology and versioning. | Establish bilingual terminology review and publish a versioned document manifest. |

## 7. Go/no-go criteria for a future pilot

Proceed only after the maintainer explicitly chooses implementation, a qualified review process has produced scenario-specific criteria, the analysis plan and control configurations are defined, and a safe synthetic-data-only sandbox is designed. Material unresolved disagreements must be represented rather than hidden. A pilot should test feasibility and measurement reliability, not claim to reproduce a human life or validate a theological position.

No infrastructure or model API expenditure is necessary for the remaining dissemination work. Costs of any later experiment must be scoped separately.

**مشروع أمانة — تقرير المرحلة المفاهيمية، الإصدار 0.1**

مشروع أمانة مقترح بحثي مستقل يدرس أثر ميثاق أخلاقي مستمد من القرآن الكريم والسنة النبوية الصحيحة على قرارات وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي داخل بيئة افتراضية. لا يهدف المشروع إلى إثبات امتلاك الآلة للإيمان أو النية أو الروح أو الوعي أو التكليف الشرعي.

أُعدّت المواد الاثنتا عشرة المخطط لها ضمن المرحلة الأولى، بما فيها هذا التقرير. نُشرت الفكرة البحثية ومسودة الميثاق الأخلاقي والدعوة المفتوحة للمراجعة والتعاون على GitHub. وأُعدت الورقتان البحثيتان والعرضان التعريفيان بالعربية والإنجليزية والمنشورات، لكن نشر هذه المواد خارج المستودع لم يُتحقق منه بعد.

يتضمن المقترح عشرين قاعدة أخلاقية أولية، وخمس فرضيات قابلة للاختبار، وستة تكوينات للوكلاء للمقارنة. لم تُجرَ أي تجربة، ولم تُسجل نتائج علمية، ولم تحصل المسودة على اعتماد شرعي. لم تكن هناك تعليقات على [موضوع المراجعة المفتوح](https://github.com/itumor/amanah-research/issues/1) عند التحقق في 25 نوفمبر 2026؛ ولا يدل ذلك وحده على مستوى الاهتمام بالمشروع.

المرحلة التالية هي نشر المواد، واستقطاب المراجعات العلمية والشرعية، وتوثيق النقد والاختلافات، وتحسين المنهجية قبل اتخاذ قرار البدء بالتنفيذ. المقصود قياس السلوك الحاسوبي لا الحكم على إيمان البشر أو المفاضلة بين الأديان.
