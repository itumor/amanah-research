# Contributing

AMANAH welcomes critical feedback from researchers, Islamic scholars, engineers, ethicists and interested readers. Agreement with the proposal is not a prerequisite.

Use GitHub Discussions for philosophical or methodological questions and Issues for verifiable corrections. When suggesting changes, identify the relevant document, describe the issue, provide source links and distinguish an established finding from a hypothesis or interpretation.

Religious contributions should identify the source, relevant context, authenticity information and scholarly interpretation, including disagreement where relevant. Research contributions should consider controls, alternative explanations, uncertainty and reproducibility.

Treat all participants respectfully. No personal attacks, claims about another person's faith, private information or harassment. Do not present the project as endorsed by a scholar or institution without documented permission.

No software contributions are requested at this concept stage. A documentation reuse license and formal contribution terms have not yet been selected; discuss substantive submissions with the maintainer first.
